About Sprachlabor

Sprachlabor offers structured German training for learners who want measurable progress, linguistic precision, and long-term retention.

Founded by a linguist with over 25 years of experience across European and Asian languages, Sprachlabor is built on firsthand knowledge of what it takes to learn structurally complex languages—and what learners need when progress feels slow or inconsistent.

The work combines linguistic expertise with practical experience in teaching, translation, writing, and language technology.

Approach

Sprachlabor follows a structured approach to language learning based on progression, pattern recognition, and systematic reinforcement.

The approach is shaped not only by teaching experience, but by the perspective of a lifelong language learner.

Rather than relying on purely communicative methods, teaching focuses on building a stable foundation that allows learners to actively use and retain the language over time.

This approach is supported by curated materials, targeted practice, and continuous feedback.

For learners who want a more intensive and structured experience, selected programmes are available within the AFLS (AntiFragile Learning System), which integrates guided review, accountability, and extended learning materials.

What sets Sprachlabor apart

  • Curated Materials
    Each lesson is built from carefully selected resources aligned with clear learning objectives.
  • Structured Learning Design
    Content is organised to support progression, retention, and active use.
  • Research-Informed Approach
    Teaching draws on established principles of memory, repetition, and skill development.
  • AI-Enhanced Teaching & Training
    Modern tools are used to support instruction, feedback, and efficient learning processes.
  • Tailored Learning Paths
    From structured foundations to advanced precision and professional language, programmes are adapted to the learner’s goals.

Background & Expertise

Sprachlabor combines over 25 years of teaching experience with academic training in Japanese and Korean Studies and Cultural Anthropology.

Courses are informed by work with learners across a wide range of professional contexts, including project management, finance, and healthcare.

In addition, Sprachlabor is a CIOL-certified translator, reflecting a high level of linguistic precision, analytical language competence, and professional standards.
