This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies and how you can manage your preferences. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to ensure website functionality and improve user experience.

2. Types of cookies we use

a) Strictly necessary cookies

Essential for website operation and set without consent.

b) Functional cookies

Store preferences such as language settings. Used with consent where required.

c) Analytics cookies

Help us understand website usage. Used only with consent and processed in aggregated form where possible.

d) Third-party cookies

Some services (e.g. video platforms or booking tools) may set cookies. We do not control these and recommend reviewing their policies.

3. Legal basis

Strictly necessary cookies do not require consent. All other cookies are used only with your consent.

4. Managing consent

You can accept, reject, or customise cookies via our cookie banner. Preferences can be changed at any time.

5. Browser controls

You can manage or delete cookies via your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect site functionality.

6. Updates

This policy may be updated. The latest version will always be available on this page.
