Diagnostic Session

A structured starting point for your German learning.

The diagnostic session is a one-to-one consultation designed to assess your current level, clarify your goals, and define the most effective learning path.

You receive a clear assessment of your level and guidance on the most effective learning approach, including whether a more structured pathway such as AFLS would be beneficial.

What it is

A structured 30-minute consultation to assess your German level, clarify your goals, and define the most effective learning path.

Ideal for learners who want clarity, structure, and a well-defined starting point.

What this session gives you

  • Clear CEFR-based level assessment (A0–C1)
  • Analysis of grammar, structure, and expression
  • Identification of strengths and priority areas
  • Personalised learning recommendations
  • Guidance on the most suitable learning pathway (including structured AFLS options where appropriate)

Why a diagnostic matters

Most learners struggle not because of effort, but because of unclear direction. This session ensures you start at the right level with the right method.

I design these sessions for people who are actively investing in their German and want to make meaningful progress through structured, efficient learning—not additional workload.
